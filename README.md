- Agentic AI : an AI system that operates autonomously to accomplish specific goals with minimum human intervention.
- Agentic AI Agent: An AI system that uses a LLM as its brain to reason, plan and take action autonomously to achieve a complex goal.
  

---

| - | Gen AI | Agentic AI |
|-----|--------|--------|
|Focus on| output quality & creativity | Achieving real-world goals through action|
|Use when | for creativity, ideas or content | You have a clear goal|
|key question | can it produce good content? | can it accomplish the objective autonomously? |
|Limitations| produces output and stops - dosen't check if goal is achieved | loops continuously - takes action, evaluates result, adapts, retries untill goal is met |

---

> Agentic AI = Gen AI + Planning + Tool Use + Feedback Loop
